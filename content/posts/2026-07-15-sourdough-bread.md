I've been baking sourdough most weekends for the past year, and I finally have
a routine reliable enough to write down.

## The starter

Feed it 1:1:1 (starter:flour:water) the night before. By morning it should
have roughly doubled and pass the float test.

## The dough

- 500g bread flour
- 350g water
- 100g active starter
- 10g salt

Mix, rest 30 minutes, then do four sets of stretch-and-folds, 30 minutes apart.
Bulk ferment until it's grown by about 50%.

![A fresh sourdough loaf cooling on a wire rack](/static/images/posts/sourdough-loaf.jpg)

## The bake

Dutch oven, lid on, 30 minutes at 240°C, then lid off for another 15-20
minutes until the crust is deep brown.

That's it — no exotic technique, just consistency.
